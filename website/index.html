How it works

  1. 1Pick your topics

    Type anything: “AI”, “Premier League transfers”, “Nigeria”. Up to eight. Owiwi pulls fresh headlines from Google News, free and with no news key.

  2. 2Bring your AI key

    Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible service. Free tiers work. One request per digest. Get a key in 2 minutes.

  3. 3Read two digests a day

    Morning and evening, up to ten cards each. Swipe up through the gist, tap Read more for the full story, and finish to keep your streak alive.

Owiwi reads along with you

Your owl reacts to your day: thinking while it writes your digest, celebrating when you finish.

Private by design

Owiwi has no server. Your phone fetches the headlines and talks to your AI provider directly. That's the whole system.

  • Your key stays on your phone

    Stored in the Android Keystore, never in a database, log or error message. After you save it, the app never shows it again.

  • No accounts, no analytics

    No sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Nothing about you is sent anywhere except the headlines your AI summarizes.

  • Grounded in real articles

    Every card must link to an article Owiwi actually fetched. Anything the AI makes up is dropped before you see it.

  • Free news sources

    Google News RSS by default, with Bing News and publisher feeds as options. No paid news APIs, no news keys.

Install on Android

Owiwi isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install the app file (APK) directly. It takes about a minute.

  1. Download the APK

    On your phone, tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns that the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.

  2. Allow installs from your browser

    When you open the file, Android asks for permission to Install unknown apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

    Can't find it? Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps › your browser. Names vary a little by phone.

  3. Open the file and install

    Tap the download notification, or open Files › Downloads › owiwi.apk, then tap Install.

  4. If Play Protect speaks up

    Because Owiwi is installed outside the Play Store, Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognize the developer. Tap More details, then Install anyway. If it offers to scan the app, that's fine too.

  5. Open Owiwi

    A short setup asks for your topics, your digest times, your AI provider and permission for reminders. You can switch Install unknown apps back off afterwards.

Requirements: Android 7.0 or newer on a 64-bit (arm64) phone, which covers nearly every phone from the last several years. About 35 MB.

Updating: download the latest APK and install it over the old one. Your topics, providers and streak stay.

Stay safe: only download Owiwi from this page or the project's GitHub releases.

Set up your AI

Owiwi uses your own AI provider to write each digest. Pick one below to see how to get a key and what to enter in the app.

New to this? Start with Gemini. It has a free tier, needs only a Google account, and the app's suggested model works as is. Groq is a good free alternative.

Google Gemini Recommended

Free tier through Google AI Studio, with daily limits and no card needed. Owiwi makes one request per digest, so it usually fits. Check Google's terms for how free-tier data may be used.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key (accept the terms if asked).
  3. Copy the key.
Suggested modelgemini-3.5-flash-lite

In Owiwi: choose Gemini, paste the key, tap Test connection, then Save provider.

Groq

Free tier with rate limits, no card needed. Very fast open models.

  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign up or sign in.
  2. Tap Create API Key and give it a name, like “Owiwi”.
  3. Copy the key now. Groq shows it only once.
Suggested modelllama-3.3-70b-versatile

In Owiwi: choose Groq, paste the key, tap Test connection, then Save provider.

OpenRouter

Free models: any model whose ID ends in :free costs nothing, with daily request limits. One key also unlocks paid models from many vendors if you add credit.

  1. Open openrouter.ai/keys and sign in.
  2. Tap Create Key and name it.
  3. Copy the key now. It is shown only once.
Suggested modelgoogle/gemma-4-31b-it:free

In Owiwi: choose OpenRouter, paste the key, tap Test connection, then Save provider.

OpenAI

Pay as you go. Add a little prepaid credit first. The suggested model is OpenAI's cheapest, and two digests a day use very little.

  1. Sign in at platform.openai.com/api-keys.
  2. Add credit under Billing if your account has none.
  3. Tap Create new secret key and copy it. It is shown only once.
Suggested modelgpt-6-luna

In Owiwi: choose OpenAI, paste the key, tap Test connection, then Save provider.

Anthropic (Claude)

Pay as you go. Buy prepaid credits in the Anthropic Console. The suggested Haiku model is the fast, low-cost one.

  1. Sign in at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys.
  2. Add credits under Billing if your account has none.
  3. Tap Create Key, name it and copy it. It is shown only once.
Suggested modelclaude-haiku-5-5

In Owiwi: choose Anthropic, paste the key, tap Test connection, then Save provider.

Custom OpenAI-compatible

Cost depends on the service. Use this for DeepSeek, Together, a self-hosted server, or anything else that speaks the OpenAI chat API over HTTPS.

  1. Create a key in your provider's dashboard.
  2. Find the base URL: the part before /chat/completions, starting with https://. For example https://api.deepseek.com/v1 or https://api.together.xyz/v1.
  3. Find the exact model ID in their docs.
Suggested modelNone. Enter your provider's model ID.

In Owiwi: choose Custom OpenAI-compatible, enter the base URL, model and key, tap Test connection, then Save provider.

Where to enter it in the app

First launch: step 4, Provider, during setup. Later: Settings › LLM providers › Add provider. The suggested model is filled in for you; change it only if you want a different one. You can add several providers and tap one to make it active.

Your key stays on your phone. Owiwi stores it in the Android Keystore and sends it only to the provider you picked. This website never sees it. Treat it like a password: don't share it, and delete it in your provider's dashboard if it leaks.

If Test connection fails

401 / 403 Key rejected
Paste the whole key again, with no spaces, and check it is still active in your provider's dashboard. For OpenAI and Anthropic, make sure the account has credit.
429 Rate limited
You hit the free tier's limit or ran out of credit. Wait a minute and try again, or check your plan.
Network error
Check your internet connection. For Custom, check the base URL starts with https:// and ends before /chat/completions.
Other errors
Usually a wrong model ID. Pick one from the model list in the app, or copy the exact ID from your provider's docs.

Questions

Is Owiwi free?

Yes. The app is free. The only possible cost is your AI provider, and Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter's free models cost nothing within their limits.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Owiwi is Android-only for now.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Owiwi is distributed directly as an APK for now. That's why Android asks you to allow the install and Play Protect may show a warning. Follow the install guide.

What data leaves my phone?

Two kinds of requests: headline searches for your topics to the news source (Google News by default), and one request per digest to your AI provider containing those headlines. No accounts, no analytics, no Owiwi server.

Does it work offline?

Once today's digest is loaded, yes. Making a new digest needs a connection. Old digests are cleared each day; bookmarks and your streak are kept.

What happens to my streak if I miss a day?

Finishing any digest counts for the day. Miss one day and Owiwi forgives it: your streak keeps going (once a week). Miss two days in a row and it starts again. An optional evening nudge can remind you before it's too late.
